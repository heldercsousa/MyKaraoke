## 💡 NAME RATIONALE

### **Why "MyVocaList":**
1. **Personal ownership**: "My" creates emotional connection with users
2. **Clear meaning**: "VocaList" = Vocal + List = Singer queue/list
3. **Universal recognition**: Works across 11 supported languages
4. **Professional sound**: More sophisticated than "MyVocaList"
5. **Broad appeal**: Works for casual singers AND professional vocalists
6. **Domain availability**: Both .com and .app were available and secured
7. **Memorable**: Easy to remember, pronounce, and spell
8. **Scalable**: Can expand beyond karaoke to general vocalist tools
